Grounded Reality

Standard map apps were not built for remote Gujarat roads.

When signals drop in rural Aravalli hills, standard commercial maps freeze or lose turn directions entirely. Yatra was engineered offline-first from day one.

Standard Navigation Apps

  • Freezes completely when 4G/5G mobile towers drop on rural highway stretches.
  • Requires mandatory Google or phone logins and collects personal travel history on remote servers.
  • Drains battery rapidly with heavy web sockets and unoptimized background polling.
  • No native convoy mode — requires switching between WhatsApp and maps while driving.

Yatra (Offline-First Architecture)

  • Caches full 40 km radius offline maps on device storage. Continues navigating smoothly in airplane mode.
  • Zero login required. All locations and trip metrics stay strictly inside local SQLite on your phone.
  • OEM battery kill prevention service logs GPS efficiently, using less than 4% battery per hour.
  • Built-in Group Yatra: share a 6-digit room code to track your whole group on one shared map screen.

Clear Answers

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know about offline functionality, group tracking, and battery optimization.

Does Yatra really navigate without any internet connection?
Yes. Before your trip, tap the download icon on the map to save a 40 km radius area around your route. Once downloaded, all road tiles, search coordinates, and turn calculations run 100% locally from your phone's storage. You can switch your phone to airplane mode and it will continue guiding you.
Do I need to create an account or provide my phone number?
No. Yatra does not require any account signup, email address, or phone number. When you open the app, a random numeric ID is generated locally. Your personal travel history stays strictly on your device.
How does Group Yatra convoy tracking work?
One member taps "Create Group" to generate a simple 6-digit code (for example, #775227). Other members enter that code under "Join Group". During the journey, members transmit lightweight coordinate pings so everyone can see relative distances on the map.
Will background tracking drain my phone's battery?
Yatra uses native Android foreground services that adjust GPS sample rates based on whether you are walking, resting, or driving. This prevents OEM battery killers from terminating the app while keeping battery consumption below 4% per hour.
Is Yatra completely free? Are there in-app ads?
Yatra is 100% free and open. There are no banner ads, popup interruptions, or paid subscriptions. It is built as a public utility for travelers across Gujarat.
