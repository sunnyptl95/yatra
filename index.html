Common Inquiries

Frequently asked questions.

Clear answers about offline functionality, group tracking, and device resources.

Does Yatra really navigate without any internet connection?
Yes. Tap the download icon to save a 40 km radius area around your route beforehand. Once downloaded, all road tiles, search coordinates, and turn calculations run 100% locally from your phone's storage. You can keep mobile data off or use airplane mode.
Do I need to create an account or provide my phone number?
No. Yatra does not require any account signup, email, or phone number. When you open the app, an anonymous local ID is generated on device. Your travel timeline stays strictly inside your phone's encrypted SQLite database.
Will background GPS tracking drain my phone's battery?
Yatra utilizes native Android foreground services that intelligently scale GPS polling based on motion (driving, resting, walking). This prevents OEM task killers while maintaining average battery consumption below 4% per hour.

Privacy Policy

1. Fundamental Privacy Principle

Yatra is engineered with an offline-first, privacy-by-design architecture. We believe personal journey records, pilgrim routes, and location data should never be collected, profiled, or monetized.

2. Zero Personal Account Information

Yatra requires no user registration. You do not need to provide an email address, telephone number, Google account, or social profile. The application functions entirely with an anonymous, locally generated numerical identifier.

3. Local Device Storage (SQLite)

All data generated during your travels—including downloaded 40 km map tile caches, saved favorite places, search queries, and journey timeline logs—is stored exclusively on your device's local file storage. This data is never synchronized to any private commercial advertising cloud.

4. Group Yatra & Ephemeral Convoy Pings

When you explicitly create or join a Group Yatra room using a 6-digit code:

  • Your device transmits periodic GPS coordinate pings to allow convoy members to view your relative position on the map.
  • These location pings are transient and are accessible only to participants who have entered the matching 6-digit room code.
  • Telemetry data is automatically discarded upon group exit or session expiration. No permanent trajectory history is logged on remote servers.

5. Device Permissions & Purpose

  • Location (Foreground / Background): Necessary solely to display your position along routes and provide voice-guided turn instructions.
  • Storage / Media: Used solely to save downloaded offline map tiles onto your local phone storage for zero-connectivity navigation.
  • Battery Optimization Exemption: Optional, to prevent OEM operating systems from terminating GPS routing while your screen is off.

6. Third-Party Services

Initial map tile downloads and highway road routing leverage open routing protocols (OSRM / OpenStreetMap contributors). These queries are executed anonymously without transmitting any user credentials or advertising cookies.

7. Contact for Inquiries

For questions regarding this privacy policy or our local data handling, you may reach out through the official support channels listed on this page.
